Help and privacy

Always open it the same way. Open the Anchored Inside app from your home screen. On iPhone, each home-screen app and Safari keep separate copies of your practice, so using the same app every time keeps your log in one place.

Your privacy. Your skills, log and recordings are saved only on this phone, in this browser. They are not sent to your therapist, this website or anyone else. If you clear your browser data or switch phones, they will be erased.

Microphone. If recording does not work, check that your browser is allowed to use the microphone in your phone's Settings.

If you are in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, call or text 988 (Suicide and Crisis Lifeline) or call 911. This page is not monitored.
