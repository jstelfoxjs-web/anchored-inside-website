Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 7, 2026

Anchored Inside Counseling Services respects your privacy. This policy explains how information may be handled when you visit this website or contact the practice.

Information You Provide

This website does not collect or store information through its contact form. When you complete the form and select “Send inquiry,” your device opens your email application with your name, email address, and message included in a prepared email to jessica@anchoredinside.com. The message is not sent until you select “Send” in your email application.

Email may not be a secure or confidential form of communication. Please do not include confidential, clinical, diagnostic, or other sensitive health information in an email. Submitting an inquiry does not establish a therapist-client relationship.

Technical Information

Website-hosting and related service providers may automatically process limited technical information, such as your IP address, browser type, device information, and pages visited, for security, performance, and website operation.

How Information Is Used

Information you voluntarily email may be used to respond to your inquiry, provide information about services, or help you connect with appropriate resources. It will not be sold or used for unrelated marketing purposes.

Sharing of Information

Information may be shared with service providers that support website hosting, email, scheduling, or business operations when reasonably necessary. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when necessary to address an immediate safety concern.

Third-Party Links

This website may link to third-party websites or services, including scheduling, insurance, and educational resources. Anchored Inside Counseling Services is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those external websites.

Emergency and Clinical Communication

This website and email address are not monitored continuously and should not be used for emergencies or urgent clinical needs. If you are in immediate danger, call 911. For crisis support in the United States, call or text 988.

Children’s Privacy

This website is intended to provide information to adults seeking services for themselves or their families. Children should not submit personal information through this website without the involvement of a parent or legal guardian.

Policy Updates

This policy may be updated as the website, services, or legal requirements change. The effective date above will reflect the most recent revision.

Contact

Questions about this website privacy policy may be directed to:
